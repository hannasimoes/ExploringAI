GuideCart AI

Shop with your list, read out by AI.

How it works

  • Write or dictate your shopping list.
  • Point the camera at a product or price label and tap Check.
  • GuideCart tells you what it thinks the product is, and asks you to confirm before ticking it off.

Please know

  • The AI can be wrong. It always says how sure it is.
  • It does not guide you around the shop or warn about obstacles. Keep using your cane or guide dog.
  • Photos are sent to an AI service to be read and are not saved by this app.

Using a screen reader?

Choose how results are spoken. You can change this later in Settings.
